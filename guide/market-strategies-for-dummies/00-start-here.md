## Welcome

This mini-book teaches you a **simple way to read charts** using the same ideas behind this research platform:

1. Figure out the big picture (going somewhere, or stuck in a range?)
2. Find an interesting price area
3. Wait for confirmation — don’t guess from one fancy candle

You will practice on **paper / demo / dry-run only** until you and the builders agree otherwise.

## How to use these lessons

- Read **one lesson**, then do the **Practice** box.
- Keep a journal (Lesson 11). That journal is gold for the developers.
- When a word is weird, peek at the [Learner glossary](99-learner-glossary.md).
- Curious about the “why” / architecture? That’s the [master guide](../README.md) — optional.

## Honesty up front

- Nothing here is financial advice.
- Past patterns do not guarantee future results.
- “Smart money” stories on the internet are **stories**. We measure price behavior.
- If a lesson and the platform disagree, **the platform rules win** for automated research.
