## The greatest hits

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Trading while “unsure” on context | Flat. Observe only. |
| Entering on a wick before the candle closes | Wait for close |
| Calling every wick a sweep | Require close back inside |
| Pattern-only entries | Pattern is a tag, not a plan |
| Moving invalidation because feelings | Invalidation is sacred on paper too |
| Revenge trade after a loss | Timer ends the session |
| Twelve markets at once | One market mastery |
| Treating Twitter intent stories as facts | Measure price; journal behavior |
| Skipping the journal on losing days | Especially journal those days |
| Asking the bot for live fire too early | Dry-run / paper until builders say otherwise |
