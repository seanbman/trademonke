## House rules (non-negotiable for this cohort)

1. **Paper / demo / dry-run only** unless a builder explicitly opens another mode in writing.
2. Never risk money you can’t lose. Ever.
3. No “all in.” Position size is small and boring on purpose.
4. If you’re emotional, you stop. Flat is a position.
5. Screenshots + journal every session (builders need evidence, not vibes).

## What “dry-run” means here

This project’s research stack is built to **fail closed** on live trading. Treat that as a feature: you’re learning to *think*, not to gamble through a half-finished robot.

## Session hygiene

Before you open a chart:

- [ ] Sleep / food / calm enough to follow a checklist  
- [ ] Timer set (e.g. 45–90 minutes max)  
- [ ] Journal doc open  
- [ ] One market (or a tiny watchlist), not twenty tabs  

After:

- [ ] Mark wins/losses on *ideas*, not ego  
- [ ] Note confusion points (those are product gold)

## Practice

Create a folder or doc titled `trading-journal-YYYY-MM`. Paste this header:

```text
Date:
Market:
Timeframe(s):
Session length:
Rule check (paper only?): Y/N
Mood 1–5:
```
