# 13. For builders — running learner cohorts

← [Mistakes](12-common-mistakes.md) · [TOC](README.md) · [Glossary →](99-learner-glossary.md)

---

This lesson is for developers who finished the [master guide](../README.md) / thesis and want **human guinea pigs** (learners) generating structured feedback.

## Cohort goal

Not “prove the strategy prints money.”  

Prove that:

1. Learners can apply Context → Location → Confirm  
2. Journals are structured enough to mine friction  
3. Insight UX / detectors disagree with humans in *interesting*, fixable ways  
4. Authority boundaries (paper/dry-run) hold under social pressure

## Suggested 10-day cohort plan

| Day | Learner focus | Builder focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Safety + journal setup | Collect baseline survey (experience, time, markets) |
| 1–2 | Candles + levels | Spot vocabulary mismatches |
| 3 | Trend vs chop | Tag regime confusion events |
| 4–5 | Three questions drills | Measure skip quality |
| 6 | FVG by eye | Compare to `imbalance.fvg` geometry later |
| 7 | Sweeps vs acceptance | Compare to sweep detector rules |
| 8 | Patterns as tags only | False-positive pattern reliance |
| 9 | Full checklist live session | Shadow: platform alerts vs human ideas |
| 10 | Retro | Promote friction → capability candidates ([thesis Ch.14](../14-hypothesis-adaptive-insight-system.md)) |

## Instrumentation (minimum)

- Shared journal sheet (Lesson 11 template)  
- Screenshot folder convention: `cohort/<id>/<date>/`  
- Weekly export: counts of Process A/B/C, skip reasons, builder_flags  
- Optional: map flags → friction event types in telemetry later

## Ethics & consent

- Learners know they are in a research/practice cohort  
- No pressure to fund live accounts  
- No shaming losing paper trades  
- Clear “stop” button: leave cohort anytime  

## What “success” looks like for CNIS

| Signal | Healthy | Unhealthy |
|---|---|---|
| Process A rate | Rising | PnL obsession, process falling |
| Confusion notes | Specific, actionable | Empty journals |
| Detector disagreement | Documented, reproducible | Hand-wavy “bot is wrong” |
| Authority | Still paper/dry-run | Social push to go live early |

## Facilitator checklist

- [ ] Master guide read (esp. Ch.2 authority, Ch.13 protocol, Ch.14 hypothesis)  
- [ ] Cohort brief sent (safety rules)  
- [ ] Journal template shared  
- [ ] Office hours scheduled  
- [ ] Explicit non-goals: no live execution, no guarantee of edge  

## Handoff back to product

Each cohort should produce a short memo:

1. Top 5 learner frictions  
2. Top 5 vocabulary mismatches (notes vs UI vs detectors)  
3. 3 capability candidates (with owners)  
4. What will **not** change (mandatory gates, dry-run)

That memo is the slow adaptation loop feeding the capability registry — humans included.
