# 1. What you will learn (and what you won’t)

← [Start here](00-start-here.md) · [TOC](README.md) · [Next: Safety →](02-safety-and-paper-trading.md)

---

## You will learn

- How to read a candlestick without panic
- How to spot levels where price often reacts
- How to tell “trending” from “going nowhere”
- A **3-question habit** before every trade idea
- What a fair value gap (imbalance) looks like
- What a “sweep” is (wick beyond a level, then reclaim)
- A few chart patterns (including rising/falling wedges) as **hints**, not magic
- A checklist and a journal format builders can study

## You will not learn (here)

- How to get rich quick
- How to override risk rules because “it feels right”
- Live order placement on this MVP stack (that’s gated and dry-run by design)
- Every ICT acronym on Twitter
- Footprint / order-flow vendor screens (advanced; see master guide later)

## Mental model

Think of trading ideas like weather reports:

| Bad habit | Better habit |
|---|---|
| “This candle means buy” | “Context + level + confirmation” |
| “Institutions did X” | “Price did X; here’s the measurement” |
| “I’m always in a trade” | “Most of the time I wait” |

## Practice

Write three sentences in your notes:

1. Why you want to learn this  
2. How much time you can practice per day  
3. Your rule: “I only paper trade until ___”
