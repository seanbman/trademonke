# Priority rooms

## 1. Scenarios (Section K) — fix first

Blank or partial on K1, K2, K4, K5. K3 (don’t widen stops emotionally) was good — keep that.

| Miss | What to practice |
|------|------------------|
| K1 | Higher-timeframe trend sets bias; lower-timeframe red into HTF support is often a pullback, not an auto-short |
| K2 | “Overbought” / high RSI into a news rip is not a short plan by itself |
| K4 | Thin break + reclaim = failed breakdown → cancel the short thesis or wait; don’t tool-hop first |
| K5 | Cutting winners and letting losers run destroys expectancy even if win rate looks fine |

**Drill:** Three charts a day. Fill the sentence pack above. No trade idea without invalidation.

## 2. Classical patterns (Section D)

Shapes were partly right. Missing pieces: confirmation, invalidation, and “pattern ≠ guaranteed outcome.”

For each pattern, memorize four fields: **shape · bias · confirmation · invalidation**.

| Pattern | Confirmation idea | Invalidation idea |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Double top | Neckline break holds | Reclaim + new high |
| Double bottom | Neckline break holds | Lose the lows / failed breakout |
| Head & shoulders (top) | Neckline failure | Strong reclaim / new high |
| Ascending triangle | Break and hold above flat top | Break below rising support |
| Descending triangle | Break and hold below flat bottom | Break above descending resistance |
| Flag | Continuation after pole + tight pause | Break opposite the pole’s direction |

## 3. Multi-timeframe (Section G + C8)

You weighted the lower timeframe too heavily.

- Higher timeframe = bias / map  
- Lower timeframe = entry timing only  
- A 15m downtrend can be a pullback inside a daily uptrend — both can be “true” on their scale  
- Lower timeframes = more signals and more noise  

**Drill:** Before any entry idea, write one line: `HTF bias = up / down / range / unsure`. If unsure, flat.

## 4. Risk language (I7, I8)

- **1R** = the dollar amount you accept losing if price hits your **original** stop  
- **Drawdown** hurts capital and decision quality; large losses need much larger gains to recover  

Journal paper trades as `+2R` / `-1R`, not only win/loss.

# Four-week plan

Paper/demo only. About 30–45 minutes, five days a week.

### Week 1 — Patterns

One pattern per day from the table above. Find one historical example that worked and one that failed. Write confirmation + invalidation for each.

### Week 2 — Timeframes

One market daily. Mark HTF bias first, then check whether LTF action is with-trend or a pullback. Re-answer C8, G1, G3, G4 from memory mid-week.

### Week 3 — Scenarios

Three charts daily with the full sentence pack. Replay K1–K5 until answers are fast and complete (under ~3 minutes each). Log three *skipped* ideas per session.

### Week 4 — Risk + retake

Every paper idea: define 1R in dollars before entry. Retake sections **D, G, K**, plus I7/I8.

| Retake | Goal |
|--------|------|
| D | 75%+ |
| G | 80%+ |
| K | 70%+ |
| I7/I8 | Clean definitions |

If D or K still lag, repeat Weeks 1 and 3 before adding new tools (orderflow, more indicators, more markets).
