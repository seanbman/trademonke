TRANSCRIBED TRADING NOTES

PAGE 1 — INTERMEDIATE VS. PROFESSIONAL TRADER

Indication:
- Auction Market Theory: Is the market balanced or seeking discovery (trending)?
- HTF, anchored VWAP, swing structure
- Volume profile (POC, HVN, LVN)
- Footprint volume: who is in control
- Bid × ask and delta; absorption
- Order flow / auction theory

Establish context: balanced or trending.
Volume profile finds location and an LVN that overlaps an FVG.
Then use footprint volume to get confirmation—absorption or aggression—at the order block before acting.
Skipping one of these steps will lead to failed signals.

Balanced or Imbalanced Markets
Balanced markets rotate between the high and low. Imbalanced markets break out on strong aggression, leaving thin, fast-traded areas behind—zones that show up as FVGs later.
LVNs help determine which state markets are in: look for reversion trades when balanced, or continuation when imbalanced.

PAGE 2 — ORDER FLOW + GEX [?]

Anchored VWAP:
- 4H swing, session VWAP
- Market structure; fair-value line for the move in progress
- Price holding above VWAP during an up-leg, or below during a down-leg

Swing Highs / Swing Lows
- Trend vs. discovery
- SH/SL: fingerprints for auctions
- SH/SL at the same level show balance; use them to validate order blocks

Footprint Charts
A grid shows the inside of a candle: sellers hitting the bid and buyers lifting the offer. It basically breaks down what happened from open to close—the blueprint of the candle and the finest resolution of order flow.

Absorption
One side (large bids) absorbs [b/s?] aggressive selling at that price without making a new low.
On a footprint chart, it looks like an outsized size/number at the same price level across different candles.
It signals that buyers keep buying at that level and absorbing selling into that level—exactly what forms an order block.
