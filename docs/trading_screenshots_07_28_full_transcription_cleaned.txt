When scanning charts for reversals, you are looking to identify where the market transitions from a Bearish to Bullish trend (or vice versa). In Smart Money Concepts (SMC), you never try to guess the exact bottom or top. Instead, you scan for institutional manipulation followed by clear structural confirmation.

Look for this exact mechanical sequence on your charts:

1. The Liquidity Hunt (The Setup)

Price must aggressively trap retail traders before reversing.

- Look for: Price sweeping past a prominent HTF Swing High (Buy-side Liquidity) or Swing Low (Sell-side Liquidity), or running past clean Equal Highs/Lows (EQH/EQL).

- The Candle Behavior: You want to see a fast spike past that level that immediately gets rejected. The candle should leave a long wick and close back inside the previous trading range.

2. Change of Character (The Confirmation)

A reversal is not valid until the immediate market structure actively breaks.

- Look for (Bullish Reversal): After a liquidity sweep to the downside, look for price to aggressively rally and close completely above the last valid Lower High that created the lowest wick.

- Look for (Bearish Reversal): After a liquidity sweep to the upside, look for price to drop sharply and close completely below the last valid Higher Low.

- Note: This body close forms your CHoCH (Change of Character) or MSS (Market Structure Shift).

3. The Displacement & Footprints (The Entry Zone)

The move that caused the CHoCH must be explosive—showing that institutional "smart money" has taken over the market.

- Look for Unmitigated FVGs: The sharp reversal move must leave behind a wide, energetic Fair Value Gap. If the market moves slow and choppy without leaving imbalances, the reversal is low-probability.

- Look for Rejection/Order Blocks: Look at the origin of that explosive move. The heavily wicked candle or final opposite candle before the expansion is your institutional entry zone.

How to Execute the Trade

Once your scanner identifies a Liquidity Sweep + CHoCH + Unmitigated FVG:

1. Place a limit order at the equilibrium (50%) of the FVG or at the open of the Order/Rejection Block.

Smart Money Concepts (SMC), you need to look for a specific, mechanical sequence rather than treating individual patterns in isolation. The most reliable setups combine Liquidity Sweeps, Market Structure Shifts (CHoCH), and Unmitigated Premium/Discount Levels.

A complete, high-probability entry scan incorporates these three structural phases:

Phase 1: High-Timeframe Context (Where to Look)

Do not scan random price points. Only look for setups inside specific institutional areas on your higher timeframe (HTF) chart:

- The Trend: Identify the overall direction (Bullish or Bearish) to ensure you are trading with the institutional flow.

- Unmitigated FVGs & Order Blocks: Find Fair Value Gaps or Order Blocks that price has not touched since they were created.

- Rejection Blocks: Look at the absolute highest or lowest wicks of a structural swing point. These wicks represent aggressive liquidity clearing before a reversal.

Phase 2: The Liquidity Sweep (The Trigger)

Price must hunt liquidity before moving. When scanning, look for a sudden, aggressive move that purges an established level and immediately snaps back:

- Liquidity Sweeps: Price runs just past a clear swing high/low (Buy-Side or Sell-Side Liquidity), equal highs/lows, or a major session high/low.

- The Sweep Signal: Look for a candle that spikes past the liquidity level but closes back inside the previous trading range, leaving a long wick.

Phase 3: Lower-Timeframe Confirmation (The Execution)

Once price sweeps liquidity inside your HTF unmitigated zone, drop down to a lower timeframe (LTF) to find your mechanical entry:

1. CHoCH (Change of Character): Look for the absolute first structural break against the immediate trend. Price must aggressively close past the last valid swing high/low that led to the liquidity sweep.

2. Locate the Displacement: The CHoCH must be aggressive, leaving behind an unmitigated FVG or a fresh Order/Rejection Block.

3. The Entry Point: Set a limit order at the consecutive open of the unmitigated FVG or the equilibrium (50% level) of the fresh Rejection Block. Your stop-loss goes safely past the structural swing point created by the liquidity sweep.
