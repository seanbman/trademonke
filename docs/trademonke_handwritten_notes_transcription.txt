- Trendline breaks
- Market Structure Shift (MSS)
- Change of Character (CHoC)
- Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) events
- Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) events
- Liquidity sweeps

The purpose is to help identify potential reversals and continuation setups.
